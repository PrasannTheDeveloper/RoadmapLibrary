Dive into FastAPI backend development with this 14-week structured learning path designed for developers with some Python experience. You'll build a solid foundation in async Python, Pydantic, and SQLAlchemy, then move into authentication, caching, and testing, before advancing to layered architecture, background tasks/message queues, and containerized deployment. By the end, you'll be equipped to design, build, and deploy production-ready, high-performance FastAPI backend systems.
