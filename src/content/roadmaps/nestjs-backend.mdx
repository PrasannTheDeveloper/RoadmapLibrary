Dive into NestJS backend development with this 16-week structured learning path designed for developers who already know TypeScript and Node.js basics. You'll build a solid foundation in Nest's modular architecture, dependency injection, and databases, then move into authentication, caching, and testing, before advancing to CQRS, microservices, message queues, and containerized deployment. By the end, you'll be equipped to design, build, and deploy production-ready, enterprise-grade NestJS backend systems.
